# @therentroll — Monetization Strategy

How the faceless Rent-Roll Instagram account turns reach into revenue. Grounded in
the content system already built (`instagram-reels-*.md`, `instagram-content-queue.md`)
and Keywise's business (California landlord-compliance SaaS; ICP: 4–10 unit landlords
switching off Excel + Venmo).

> Note: this is the strategy layer. The account is **owned media** — its first job is
> to feed Keywise, not to chase ad pennies. Diversify only after the funnel works.

## The core thesis
The account's most valuable output isn't views — it's **qualified landlords on an email
list**. Instagram rents you attention; email and a product own it. So every revenue path
routes through one funnel:

**Reel (reach) → bio link → free lead magnet → email list → Keywise trial + affiliate offers.**

Everything below hangs off that spine.

## Revenue streams, sequenced by audience size
Don't run all of these at once. Unlock them in order — earlier ones fund and de-risk later ones.

### Stage 1 — 0–1,000 followers: build the funnel (revenue: ~$0, that's fine)
- **Goal:** prove the Reel → email pipeline, not to earn yet.
- **Lead magnet:** a genuinely useful free download tied to top-performing content — e.g. the
  "5 Lease Clauses That Pay for Themselves" checklist (Reel 4) or a rent-tracking template.
  Delivered by email opt-in (link in bio via a simple landing page or Beehiiv/ConvertKit).
- **Bio link:** one link → landing page with the lead magnet + a soft Keywise mention.
- **KPI:** email opt-in rate per 1k views (target ≥1–2%).

### Stage 2 — 1k–10k: turn on the Keywise funnel + first affiliates
- **Keywise (primary):** the account's home-team offer. Free for 1–2 units, **Pro $49/mo
  ($29/mo founding, $390/yr)**. Warm the list with the compliance angle (AB 1482, security
  deposits, just-cause), then trial CTAs. Track with UTM links so signups are attributable.
- **Affiliate partnerships (secondary):** promote tools landlords already buy, with honest
  reviews only. Natural fits: tenant-screening services, rent-payment platforms, landlord
  insurance, LLC formation, accounting/tax tools. Disclose every affiliate link (FTC).
- **Email newsletter:** weekly landlord tips → the list becomes its own channel, monetizable
  independent of the algorithm.
- **KPI:** trial starts/mo, trial→paid rate, affiliate EPC (earnings per click).

### Stage 3 — 10k+: digital products + sponsorships
- **Digital products (highest margin):** package the content into paid assets —
  a lease-clause pack, a landlord bookkeeping spreadsheet, a "new landlord" mini-course,
  state-specific compliance checklists. $9–79 range; sold via Gumroad/Stripe.
- **Sponsorships / brand deals:** once reach is consistent, charge for integrated Reels.
  Only accept sponsors relevant to landlords; never sponsor a competitor to Keywise.
- **KPI:** product revenue/mo, sponsor CPM, refund rate.

## What NOT to do
- **Don't lead with ads/sponsorships early** — it caps trust before you've built any, and
  the audience is too small to pay meaningfully.
- **Don't sell a course before the free funnel converts** — a lead magnet that doesn't drive
  email signups won't drive $49 sales.
- **Don't promote anything you wouldn't use** — this is a compliance brand; credibility is the
  whole asset.

## Economics (rough, for planning)
- Keywise Pro LTV drives the model: even a **1% view→trial** and modest trial→paid makes the
  Keywise funnel worth more than affiliate/ad revenue at this scale.
- Affiliate/products are **diversification + resilience**, not the main line, until 10k+.
- Prioritize the stream with the best **revenue per 1,000 views (RPM)** once you have data —
  early on that's almost always the Keywise funnel, because you own the product margin.

## Guardrails (non-negotiable)
- **FTC disclosure** on every affiliate link and sponsored post ("#ad", "paid partnership").
- **No misleading income/【legal claims** — it's a compliance brand; keep the "not legal/tax
  advice" posture from the content docs.
- **Don't sell user data**, and never post real tenant/rent-roll data (same rule as content).
- Keep promotional posts a **minority of the feed** (≈1 in 5); the value content is what earns
  the right to sell.

## 90-day activation plan
1. **Weeks 1–4:** ship Reels (existing kit), stand up the bio landing page + one lead magnet,
   start the email list. Measure opt-in rate.
2. **Weeks 5–8:** add Keywise trial CTAs to the email sequence + a few Reels; add UTM tracking;
   test one relevant affiliate.
3. **Weeks 9–12:** review RPM by stream; double down on the winner; draft the first digital
   product if the list is converting.
